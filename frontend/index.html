IMPORTANT / RESEARCH SAFETY

Research output is not a medical diagnosis.

This platform is an experimental research and software demonstration. Its AI outputs are not clinically validated and must not be used to diagnose, exclude, or treat a brain condition.

  • MobileNetV2 is an experimental binary classifier, not a clinical decision system.
  • Its reported sensitivity is limited; a low tumor probability does not rule out a tumor.
  • It does not determine tumor location, type, grade, or treatment.
  • All medical imaging must be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional.
